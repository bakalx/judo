> Hold each stretch passively for **45–60 seconds**. Breathe deeply to shift out of sympathetic fight-or-flight after hard sparring.

### **90/90 Hip Rotator Stretch**

Sit with both legs bent at 90-degree angles. Lean forward over the front knee with a flat back, then sit tall and rotate the rear hip internally.  
(_Why:_ Frees internal and external hip rotation so your pelvis can pivot cleanly on _tai-sabaki_ (body turns) without twisting through the lumbar spine.)

![[Pasted image 20260909220249.png]]

**The Couch Stretch:** Place one shin flush against a wall with the knee on the mat, stepping the opposite foot forward into a lunge. Squeeze the glute on the back leg and drive the pelvis forward without overarching your back.
    
    - _Why:_ Combats the tight hip flexors caused by defending low-hip attacks like drop _Seoi-nage_, which otherwise pulls the pelvis into an anterior tilt and crushes the lower back.
        
- **Frog Stretch:** Spread knees wide on the tatami, shins parallel to each other, feet pointed outward, and sink hips straight back onto the elbows.
    
    - _Why:_ High-yield adductor length. Vital protection against groin tears when your leg gets caught or swept unexpectedly.
