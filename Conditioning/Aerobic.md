## Endurance & Base Building

- **Running (Zone 2 Steady-State):** A continuous 45-to-60-minute run at a strictly conversational pace, focusing on nasal breathing to build mitochondrial density and capillary beds without excessive recovery cost.
    
- **RowErg (Long Steady Distance):** A 30-to-45-minute continuous row at a low, controlled stroke rate (18–20 spm) with a moderate drag factor, keeping your heart rate flat in the aerobic zone.
    
- **Stairs (Continuous Low-Cadence Climb):** A 30-minute continuous session on a Stairmaster or stadium steps at a slow, measured cadence that maintains a steady aerobic heart rate without triggering heavy muscular fatigue.
