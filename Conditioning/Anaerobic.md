## Power Output & Lactate Tolerance

- **Running (VO2 Max Intervals):** 6 x 400-meter sprints at a near-maximal, repeatable pace (around 90-95% effort), with 90 seconds of active walking or slow jogging recovery between sets.
    
- **RowErg (High-Intensity Power Intervals):** 8 x 500-meter all-out intervals with 90 seconds of complete rest, training maximal power output and building high-end glycolytic capacity.
    
- **Stairs (Explosive Stair Sprints):** Max-effort sprints up a flight of stairs (skipping every second step to challenge hip extension and explosive power), followed by a slow walk down for recovery, repeated for 8 to 10 rounds.
